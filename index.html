How to play

Clear 3 rooms.

        
        
Use the paper’s clues and monsters’ moves to find every target. The last room targets the king.

        
Capture them all, then take the stairs from anywhere. Stairs cost no moves.

        
After a win or reset, practice with any piece and color. Only the official piece and color earn a shareable result.

      

      

        
Tap your piece for legal moves. Land on a monster to capture it.

        

          
          
          
          
          
          
        

        
MoveCapture

        
Obstacles block paths; knights jump. Pawns may open with two steps; the far edge promotes them.

        
Keyboard
Arrows / WASD: select · Enter: move · Esc: clear · Tab: controls

      

      

        

          
White: you → enemies

          
Black: enemies → you

        

        
        
Red paths warn of attacks. Black may be hit before moving. Destination blocked? Choose again; enemies wait.

Each hit costs a life. Zero lives restarts the dungeon. A hit undoes pawn promotion.

      

      

        
Coins and optional captures earn gold; challenge pieces pay nothing. After each of the first two floors, the stairs open a shop: buy powerups or reserve a companion for the next floor. You start with no gold. With no shop after it, the last floor holds no coins.
