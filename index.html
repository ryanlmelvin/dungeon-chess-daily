Keyboard
Arrows / WASD: select · Enter: move · Space: wait · Esc: clear · Tab: controls
Then your recruit: arrows + Enter: move it · Space: keep it still

      

      
      

        
        
Clear 3 floors. On each, capture every target to open the door, then take the stairs. The last floor’s target is the king.

        
The fewer moves, the better.

      

      

        
        
Every monster is a chess piece in disguise. Look-alikes move alike: watch them to spot the targets.

        
Each capture reveals a monster’s piece.

      

      

        
        
A red path shows where a monster will capture next: step off it.

        
Up close there’s no warning: next to a monster, or a knight’s jump from one, you can be captured at once. Being captured costs a life.

      

      

        

          
          
          
          
          
          
        

        
MoveCapture

        
Tap your piece to see its moves. Pawns may open with two steps, and a pawn on the last rank becomes a queen for the floor.

      

      

        
Your daily piece is white: you move, then the monsters. In practice you can play black, and they move first.

        
Tap Wait to skip a move. Far-off queens, rooks and bishops step closer and show a red path before they capture.

Lose them all and you’re back to floor 1.

      

      

        
        
Step off a red path and the monster still lunges, onto the square you left. Stars mean it’s off-balance: it can’t capture you until your next move ends.

        
Bait a lunge, sidestep, then strike!

      

      

        
Coins and extra captures pay 1 gold each; targets pay nothing. Spend gold in the shop after floors 1 and 2.

        
Opening the door cracks the ceiling. Gold you grab after that is at risk: if the ceiling caves in before you take the stairs, you drop it.

      

      

        

          
Bone ward: blocks one capture.

          
Frost hourglass: freezes every monster for 3 moves.

          
Coin magnet: grabs coins within 2 squares.

          
Pin: freezes one monster for 3 moves.

          
Borrowed move: one knight move (a queen move, if you’re a knight).

          
Take-back: undo your last move.

          
Extra life: one more life.

        

      

      

        
        
Each floor’s recruit waits in a green ring. Step onto it and it joins you.

        
After each move, tap a green square to move it, or tap it to stay put. It can capture targets too, except on Auto (in the menu), where it moves itself.

      

      

        

          
          
        

        
As in chess: a pawn that steps two squares past an enemy pawn can be captured as if it had stepped one, on the very next move only. It works both ways.

      

      

        
        
Your rook can castle with a king recruit, as in chess: same row, two open squares between, not out of, through or into check. Tap the king.

        
A practice king castles with a rook recruit: tap where the king lands.

      

      

        
        
When your bishop recruits a bishop, the recruit hops to a side square on the other colour (on Manual, tap the green one). Together they cover every square.

      

      

        

          
          
        

        
Fork 🔱 Attack two or more monsters at once, then capture one on your very next move: +2 gold.

        
Combo 🔥 Capture on moves in a row. The third in a row, and each one after, pays +1 gold.

      

      

        
Some days add one modifier to every floor. Tap its name above the board for a reminder.

        
🧊 Ice

        
End a move on ice and your piece skids one more square the same way, if it’s open. Knights and kings don’t skid, and captures stop you. Monsters never skid.

        
🧱 Collapse

        
Every 10 moves, a new monster walks in at the door. At most 3 per floor, and none once the stairs open.

      

      

        
Each story in the paper describes one floor’s target. Stuck? Tap Hint: the first is free, the next two add +1 move each.

        
Par is about how many moves a careful player needs. Your count stops at the last target.

        
A bot clears every daily before it’s published: after you win, watch its run. Win or lose, you can then practice with any piece.
