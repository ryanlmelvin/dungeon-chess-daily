Field notes

    
 · Practice

    
Capture a monster to open the stairs. Escape through three rooms. Tap a square to move. Tap your piece to see legal moves. Capture a monster by landing on it.
Your piece follows chess movement. Pawns move forward into empty squares and capture only diagonally. Reach the far rank to become a queen.
Monsters have chess moves too. Watch them and read the newspaper. A red path warns where an enemy can capture on its next turn.
Every move advances the monsters. You have 60 moves. Coins and extra captures improve your edition. Getting captured ends the run. Your first result is the daily; practice never replaces it.
Keyboard
Arrow keys or WASD select a square. Enter moves there. Escape clears the selection.
