Field notes

    
 · Practice

    
Capture all enemies of the named chess role. The target group has matching sprites: 8 pawns, 2 rooks, knights, or bishops, or 1 queen or king. The newspaper hints at their disguise; watch how they move. The third floor always targets the king. Once every target is captured, click the revealed stairs from anywhere to leave.
Tap a square to move or your piece to see legal moves. Land on a monster to capture it. Pillars and crates block paths; knights can jump over them. Pawns advance into empty squares and capture diagonally. Reach the far rank to become a queen.
A red path warns of an enemy's next attack. Every move advances the monsters. You have 60 moves. Pawns start with 8 lives; rooks, knights, and bishops with 2; queens and kings with 1. Each hit costs a life unless a shield saves you. Losing your last life restarts today's dungeon. Promotion keeps your remaining lives; losing a life turns you back into a pawn.
Collect coins and earn gold from captures. Tap your gold to buy powerups; buying and taking revealed stairs cost no moves. Pickups activate immediately. Your first result is the daily; practice never replaces it.
Keyboard
Arrow keys or WASD select a square. Enter moves there or takes revealed stairs. Tab reaches the shop, stairs, and menu. Escape clears the selection.
