Background music (optional, off by default)

Every track is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Credit is not required,
but given gladly. Each edition plays three different tracks, one per floor.
Files were trimmed of edge silence, loudness-matched to -18.4 LUFS (a few
uneven tracks were gently leveled) and re-encoded as MP3.
